How You Bond

Pick the attachment style that feels most like you, and get concrete guidance for how you show up when a bond gets triggered — and the moves that carry you toward secure.

Attachment style is the pattern your nervous system learned for closeness: how you reach out, protect yourself, and repair. This tool turns that pattern into things you can actually notice and do — when you're triggered, and on the way back to steady.

This is a reflective lens for self-understanding — not a diagnosis, and not therapy or clinical advice. Curious for a plain-English primer first? The Attachment Project's free explainer is a friendly place to start. Attachment style can shift over time with safety and awareness.
