# fun

A collection of little web toys — scored quizzes that read the shape of your
choices and hand you an archetype, plus pick-your-type guides that give you
tailored advice. The landing page ([`index.html`](index.html)) is a hub that
links to each one.

## The Trolley Test

An interactive philosophy toy: 30 trolley-problem dilemmas that map your
choices across seven ethical dials and hand you a moral archetype. Lives in
[`trolley.html`](trolley.html).

## The Holiday Quiz

A travel toy: 20 questions about how you like to travel that map your choices
across six travel dials — pace, setting, budget, planning, sociability, and
curiosity — and reveal your holiday archetype (Beach Lounger, City & Culture
Explorer, Adrenaline Adventurer, and more), plus where you'd thrive. Lives in
[`holiday.html`](holiday.html).

## The Money Mind

A money toy: 18 questions about how you spend, save, and gamble that map your
choices across six money dials — flow, horizon, risk, driver, signal, and
control — and hand you a money archetype (The Optimizer, The Free Spirit, The
Guardian, The Baller, and more), plus the blind spot you'd rather not admit to.
Lives in [`money.html`](money.html).

## The Decision Dial

A decision toy: 18 scenarios about how you actually make the call, mapped across
six dials — gut vs analysis, fast vs slow, solo vs consensus, safe vs bold,
heart vs head, flexible vs committed — revealing your decision-making archetype
(The Strategist, The Gut-Caller, The Deliberator, and more). Lives in
[`decisions.html`](decisions.html).

## Work Mode

A work toy: 18 questions about how you do your best work, mapped across six
dials — deep vs collaborative, structure vs autonomy, lark vs owl, steady vs
sprint, plan vs improvise, backstage vs spotlight — and your work archetype,
plus the environments where you thrive. Lives in [`work.html`](work.html).

## Conflict Style

A conflict toy: 18 tense moments mapped across six dials — assertive vs
accommodating, direct vs tactful, hot vs cool, win vs harmony, confront vs
avoid, principled vs pragmatic — revealing how you really handle friction
(Thomas-Kilmann-style archetypes from The Competitor to The Peace Broker).
Lives in [`conflict.html`](conflict.html).

## Care Styles

A care toy: 18 questions about how you give and receive care, mapped across six
dials — words vs actions, time vs things, touch vs space, private vs open,
steady vs grand, give vs receive — and your care archetype, plus how you feel
most loved. Lives in [`care.html`](care.html).

## The Taste Test

An aesthetics toy: 18 "which draws your eye?" moments mapped across six dials —
minimal vs maximal, classic vs novel, cosy vs bold, muted vs vivid, ordered vs
organic, function vs form — that name your aesthetic (The Minimalist, The
Maximalist, The Colourist, and more). Lives in [`taste.html`](taste.html).

## The Enneagram Navigator

Pick your Enneagram type and wing to get concrete guidance for what happens
when you're stressed — and how to actually unwind — based on your type's
stress (disintegration) and growth (integration) arrows, coloured by the
chosen wing. Lives in [`enneagram.html`](enneagram.html).

## The Chronotype Navigator

Pick your sleep chronotype — Lion, Bear, Wolf, or Dolphin — to get a daily
schedule that works with your natural energy: when to do deep work, exercise,
cut the caffeine, wind down, and sleep. Tune the advice toward better sleep or
more focus. Lives in [`chronotype.html`](chronotype.html).

## The Attachment Navigator

Pick your attachment style — Secure, Anxious, Avoidant, or Disorganized — to see
how you show up when you're triggered, and the concrete moves that carry you
toward secure functioning. Tailor it to romance or friendships. Lives in
[`attachment.html`](attachment.html).

## The Care Languages Navigator

Pick your primary care language — Words, Acts, Time, Gifts, or Touch — and learn
to recognise and speak the other four, so care actually lands with a partner,
friends, or family. Lives in [`care-languages.html`](care-languages.html).

## The Type Compass

Pick your four-letter type (all sixteen) to see how you fall into your
stress "grip" — and the growth path that pulls you back out — coloured by the
Turbulent or Assertive identity. Lives in [`type-compass.html`](type-compass.html).

### GitHub Pages

The site deploys automatically via the workflow in
[`.github/workflows/pages.yml`](.github/workflows/pages.yml). To enable it,
go to **Settings → Pages** and set the **Source** to **GitHub Actions**. Once
`main` is updated, the site publishes at:

```
https://clementtay.github.io/fun/
```

Each toy is a self-contained HTML file, so it's also reachable directly — e.g.
`https://clementtay.github.io/fun/holiday.html`.
